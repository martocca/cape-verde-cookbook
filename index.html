Why this cookbook exists

To make Cape Verdean food easier to discover.

This cookbook is designed for people who may be unfamiliar with Cape Verdean cuisine. The recipes are approachable guides, while the cultural sections show how ingredients and dishes connect to island life, family and tradition.

01

Island food

Cape Verdean cuisine reflects African and Portuguese influences alongside the ingredients available across a dry Atlantic archipelago.

The ingredient that keeps returning

Maize is part of the story.

Maize is a recurring foundation in Cape Verdean cooking. Dishes such as cachupa and xerém connect an everyday staple to larger traditions of preparation, family meals and gatherings.
